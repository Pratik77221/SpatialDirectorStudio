# Spatial Director Studio Demo — Design Truth

## Concept angle

Present the product as a compact spatial production control room: every screen is real evidence, while the surrounding motion language behaves like precise film-set telemetry.

## Visual identity

- Background: near-black navy `#07090d` with a subtle architectural grid and restrained vignette.
- Primary text: warm white `#f5f2ea`.
- Secondary text: cool grey `#9aa4b2`.
- Action accent: amber `#f2c94c` for director controls, active shots, and capture state.
- System accent: cyan `#58d5ff` for timing, connectivity, and delivery state.
- Surfaces: opaque charcoal panels with thin neutral borders; no milky glass treatment.
- Evidence frame: rounded 28px viewport with a 1px highlight, deep shadow, and a 16:9 editorial crop.

## Typography

- Display: Arial Black / Arial, heavy and tightly tracked, used only for scene theses.
- Interface and body: Arial, regular/medium.
- Telemetry: Consolas, tabular figures, uppercase labels.
- Titles must be visibly larger than supporting copy; eyebrow, headline, and detail text use distinct sizes and weights.

## Composition

- Focal element: the real Lens Studio or dashboard evidence viewport.
- Edge anchors: top-left chapter number and product name; top-right running sequence marker.
- Supporting detail: one concise bottom-left thesis and one bottom-right fact chip.
- Background: geometric grid, one amber/cyan glow, and a thin progress rail. Decorative layers must never reduce UI legibility.

## Motion

- Evidence viewports use `multi-phase-camera`: controlled settle, then a slow push-in with minimal drift.
- Title words use `waterfall-entry`: binary arrival from below with diminishing gaps.
- Product status changes use `control-target-sync`: timing and state markers change in the same beat as the evidence cut.
- Hero glows use `ambient-glow-bloom` with opacity below 0.30.
- Scene changes are editorial hard cuts; motion happens inside scenes, preserving the feel of a directed demo.

## Exact take timing

- The director sequence starts at global time 36.0s and ends at 48.0s.
- Camera 1: 36.0–40.0s.
- Camera 2: 40.0–44.0s.
- Camera 3: 44.0–48.0s.
- The 12-second sequence is continuous and labeled `4 SEC` for each camera.
